# ShopWave-Chatbot-Project
## Introduction
Customer support has become an integral touchpoint of business and its customers. As online services grow, the number of enquiries received each day continues to grow, and many support teams find it hard to respond quickly enough to meet customer expectations. Long wait times, repetitive questions and requests outside of normal hours can put pressure on staff and often leads to a poor customer experience. To counter this, organisations are using artificial intelligence (AI) and chatbot technology to automate routine customer service tasks.
A chatbot is a software application that can interact with users and provide automated responses to customer inquiries. Modern chatbots use Natural Language Processing (NLP) to interpret user input, identify intent and generate suitable responses. These systems can respond to frequently asked questions, guide users through common processes and transfer more complex enquiries to human support agents when needed. Research shows that properly designed chatbots can help reduce response times, increase service availability and improve customer satisfaction.
The report deals with a group project that includes planning the development of an AI-powered chatbot for a medium-sized Australian online retailer ShopWave Pty Ltd. The chatbot is designed to deal with high-volume, low-complexity queries such as order status, delivery, returns and FAQs, with complex issues being escalated to human agents. The modern AI technologies and cloud based chatbot platforms are proposed to be used in the solution. As per the assignment brief, this report provides the following deliverables for Task 1: a detailed scope statement, a Gantt chart showing the interdependencies between the activities, a risk and issues management strategy and a change request management process.
